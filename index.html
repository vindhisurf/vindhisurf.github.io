FAQ

Before you come

Do I need to know how to swim?

You should be comfortable in the water. We teach in shallow whitewater first, and your instructor is a lifeguard. Tell us your level when you book.

What time is the lesson?

It depends on the tide and wind. We confirm the best time for your day on WhatsApp, usually the day before.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, water and a towel. Board and rash vest are included.

Is there a minimum age?

Children from 8 years old can join, with an adult present on the beach.

What if the sea is not safe?

We reschedule for free or refund you. Safety comes before any lesson.

How do I pay? What is the cancellation policy?

For courses and groups we ask a 50% deposit by M-Pesa or e-Mola to confirm. The rest is paid on the beach (cash, M-Pesa, e-Mola, Visa or bank transfer). Cancel more than 24h before and we refund the deposit.

Where do we meet?

At our meeting point on the beach in Ponta do Ouro. We send you the pin when we confirm. Open map

Do you do surf trips?

Trips to other breaks in southern Mozambique are in preparation. Ask us on WhatsApp to join the waiting list.
